# Art Direction: Newtonian Asteroids

## Style and Mood
- **Style**: Polished sci-fi, semi-realistic with high contrast.
- **Mood**: Cold, vast, and dangerous space.
- **Palette**: Deep blacks, blues, and purples for space; metallic grays for asteroids; vibrant cyan/orange for ship lights and projectiles.

## Key Assets
- **player_ship**: Sleek, triangular sci-fi ship, top-down view, facing forward (top).
- **asteroid**: Detailed craggy dark gray space rocks, sharp edges.
- **projectile**: Elongated glowing cyan energy bolt.
- **space_background**: Subtle nebula with distant starfield, deep dark blue tones.
- **explosion_vfx**: Circular burst of orange-red fire and white-hot center.

## Prompt Notes
- Ensure top-down perspective for all game objects.
- Background should be 16:9 for widescreen feel.
- Sprites need background removal.
