# Sound Direction: Newtonian Asteroids

## Music Mood/Energy
- **Ambient Space**: Low-energy, atmospheric drones with subtle electronic pulses. Calm but slightly tense.

## Core SFX
- **laser_fire**: Short, sharp "pew" or energy pulse (high frequency).
- **explosion**: Heavy, low-frequency crunch with some dissipating sizzle.
- **thrust**: (Procedural) Gentle white noise/hum while holding W.

## Playback Notes
- Music loops indefinitely.
- SFX triggered on fire, impact, and death.
- Initial click to start audio.
